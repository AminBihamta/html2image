Privacy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Your HTML and images

HTML is rendered in your browser and is not uploaded to this app’s server. Scripts are disabled in the isolated preview. Markup may reference external images, fonts, stylesheets, or other resources; your browser may contact those providers and disclose the usual connection information to them. Avoid including confidential content or references you do not trust.

Diagnostics

The app sends limited render diagnostics to its hosting provider: render stage, width, duration, output size, blocked image count, and error category. Pasted HTML, image URLs, and raw error messages are not included. If an image cannot be embedded, its hostname is shown locally in the status message and is not sent to the app server.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-RZM8PV32MF) is optional and is loaded only after you choose “Allow analytics”. If you decline, this app does not load Google Analytics. Your choice is stored in this browser so we can remember it.

Hosting logs

The hosting provider may process standard technical request data to deliver and protect this website. No HTML submitted to the editor is sent to the app server.
